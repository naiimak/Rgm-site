# JellyFit — Brand Brief

Use this brief, together with the attached images, to design the JellyFit logo and brand materials.

Website: https://jellyfit28.netlify.app (moving to https://jellyfit.online)

---

## 1. The product

- **What it is:** a chilled, single-serve **protein jelly cup** (100 g) with a peel-off foil lid, eaten with a spoon.
- **Headline numbers:**
  - **20 g complete protein, from whey isolate**
  - **under 1 g sugar**
  - **about 95 kcal**
  - **Halal by design**: agar base plus halal-certified bovine gelatin
- **Launch flavors:** Raspberry and Mango.
- **Positioning:** a treat-textured snack, **not a supplement**. It should feel like dessert, not like gym powder.
- **Tagline:** *"Protein you actually crave."*
- **Market:** pilot in Beirut gyms first, then the UAE, then the wider GCC.
- **Audience:** gym-goers and active people aged 18–40 who want protein without shakes, and who care about taste, sugar and halal.

## 2. Brand personality

Playful, confident, clean and premium.

- Playful: jelly wobble, a bit of fun.
- Confident: big, bold numbers.
- Clean: nothing medical or "bro-science".
- Premium: it should look like it belongs in a nice gym fridge or a café, not a supplement aisle.

## 4. Typography

- **Headings and the current wordmark:** **Nunito**, Black (900). Rounded and bold. Google Fonts, free.
- **Body text:** **DM Sans**, Regular to Bold. Google Fonts, free.
- The wordmark on the cup is a bold, rounded geometric sans in white: "JellyFit", with a capital J and capital F and no space.

## 5. Logo (chosen: "Jelly Drop")

- **Wordmark:** "JellyFit" in Nunito Black, converted to outlines. The dot of the **i** is a glossy pink jelly drop (`#E8899A`).
  - Main version: raspberry lettering on light backgrounds.
  - Reversed version: white lettering on raspberry or dark backgrounds.
- **Icon:** a lowercase **j** whose dot is the same jelly drop. White on raspberry for the app icon, favicon and profile pictures.
- Files are in `brand/logo`, `brand/icon` and `brand/social` (see `brand/README.md`).

## 6. Using the logo

1. Use the full wordmark wherever there's room: website header, packaging, posters.
2. Use the **j** icon when space is small or round: favicon, WhatsApp/Instagram profile picture, stickers, app icon.
3. Keep clear space around the logo at least the height of the jelly drop. Don't stretch, rotate or recolor it.
4. **Optional, for the GCC:** an Arabic companion wordmark, for example "جيلي فيت", with the same jelly-drop idea. Have a native speaker check the spelling.

## 7. Do / Don't

**Do**
- Keep it rounded, bold and friendly. The jelly should wobble, not look sharp.
- Use Raspberry as the hero color. Mango is the second flavor color, not the brand color.
- Leave room for the big number ("20g") to be the hero on packaging.

**Don't**
- No pills, capsules, syringes, scoops, dumbbells or flexing arms. It's a snack, not a supplement.
- Don't make it look medical, clinical or like a weight-loss product.
- Don't use collagen imagery or wording. JellyFit's protein is whey isolate.

## 8. Claim rules (for any marketing copy or packaging)

- The protein number is **whey isolate only**, always labelled **"complete protein, from whey isolate"**.
- The gelatin that sets the jelly is made from collagen. Its protein is **never counted in the 20 g**, and it is never added into one bigger protein number.
- **No skin or joint health claims.** These are regulated health claims in the GCC.
- Nutrition values are **targets** until the formula is lab-confirmed.
- Say **"Halal"** or **"Halal by design"**. Only say "certified" for the gelatin.
