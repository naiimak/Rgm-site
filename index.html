FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find what you're looking for? Drop us a line.

How do I use JellyFit?

Twist off the cap and squeeze. Most people take one pouch 15–30 minutes before training and another every 45–60 minutes during longer sessions. Recap it between sips if you don't finish it in one go.

Does it contain caffeine?

No. Every flavor in the current range is caffeine-free, so you can use it for evening training without worrying about your sleep.

Is JellyFit vegan and gluten-free?

Yes. We use plant-based gelling agents instead of gelatin, and none of our ingredients contain gluten.

How should I store it?

Keep unopened pouches in a cool, dry place. Once opened, recap, refrigerate and finish within 24 hours. It tastes best chilled.

Can I skip or cancel my subscription?

Anytime. Just email us before your next shipping date and we'll skip, pause or cancel. No fees, no awkward phone calls.
