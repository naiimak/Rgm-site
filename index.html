What is JellyFit?

A ready-to-eat raspberry jelly cup with 20g of complete protein from whey isolate, less than 1g of sugar and 95 calories. Peel off the foil lid and eat it with a spoon.

What does “complete protein” mean?

A complete protein contains all nine essential amino acids, the ones your body can't make and has to get from food. Whey protein isolate is a complete protein.

Is JellyFit halal?

Yes. JellyFit is halal, and it says so on every cup.

Is it suitable for people with a milk allergy, or for vegans?

No. JellyFit's protein comes from whey, which is made from milk. If you have a milk allergy or follow a vegan diet, JellyFit isn't for you. Always check the pack for full allergen information.

When should I eat it?

Whenever suits you. People like it after training, as a snack between meals, or as a high-protein dessert.

Anytime. Just email us before your next delivery date and we'll skip, pause or cancel. No fees.
