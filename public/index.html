The JellyFit difference

Not all protein is complete.

Many protein jellies are made with collagen. Collagen is an incomplete protein: it's missing tryptophan, one of the nine essential amino acids your body can't make on its own. JellyFit gets its protein from whey isolate, which has all nine.

What is JellyFit?

A chilled 100g jelly cup with 20g of complete protein from whey isolate, under 1g of sugar and about 95 calories. It's a snack with a treat-like texture, not a supplement.

When and where can I buy it?

We're finishing the recipe now and launching first in a handful of Beirut gyms. Join the WhatsApp list and we'll tell you where to find it.

What does “complete protein” mean?

A complete protein contains all nine essential amino acids, the ones your body can't make and has to get from food. Whey isolate is complete. Collagen, used in many protein jellies, is not: it's missing tryptophan.

Does JellyFit contain collagen?

Only a little, as the gelatin that sets the jelly. Gelatin is made from collagen. Its protein isn't counted in the 20g on the cup: that number is complete protein from whey isolate only.

Is JellyFit halal?

Yes, by design. JellyFit is set with an agar base and halal-certified bovine gelatin.

Is it vegetarian or vegan? What about allergies?

No. JellyFit contains whey, which comes from milk, and bovine gelatin. If you have a milk allergy, JellyFit isn't for you. Always check the cup for full allergen information.

Does it need to stay cold?

Yes. JellyFit is a chilled product, so keep it in the fridge. It tastes best cold anyway.
